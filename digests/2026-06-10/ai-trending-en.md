Instead of building monolithic agents, the community is now assembling skill libraries. This mirrors the early days of package management for code, but for agent behavior. The explosive star growth (3,191 for `last30days-skill`) indicates that practitioners are hungry for pre-built, domain-specific agent skills they can drop into their workflows.

**2. Local-First, Hardware-Aware AI is Going Mainstream**  
`turbovec` (Rust vector index) and `whichllm` (hardware-aware LLM benchmarking) both saw massive daily star counts. This signals a shift toward **edge deployments** where performance and resource constraints matter. The Rust-based trend for AI infrastructure (`turbovec`, `qdrant`, `meilisearch`, `rig`) continues to gain momentum – developers are choosing memory-safe, high-performance languages for the compute layer while retaining Python bindings for ease of use.

The ecosystem is coalescing around a universal agent protocol, making skills, plugins, and memory systems interoperable across different front-ends. This is a maturing sign – similar to how REST standardised web APIs.
