# ArXiv AI Research Digest — 2026-10-07

## 1. Today's Highlights

Today's submissions are dominated by three converging pushes: making reinforcement learning and world models practical for real robot behavior (QF3, DepthWorld, Parallel Predictive World Models), understanding what actually happens inside LLMs during post-training (spurious forgetting, in-parameter memory, values-vs-behavior gaps), and tightening evaluation rigor (information-theoretic conformal prediction, minimal-pair bias probes, cross-model consensus failures). Agents are maturing from "can they do it" to "can they do it cheaply, safely, and in parallel" — with new work on autonomous artifact distillation, adaptive verification, parallel coordination, and watermarking. Finally, world models are gaining modalities and grounding, moving from RGB video toward 3D geometry and synchronized sound.

## 2. Key Papers

### 🧠 Large Language Models

**Denoising Hierarchical Representations: Joint Continuous Diffusion for Language Modeling**
[arxiv.org/abs/2610.08738v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08738v1)
*Ollu & Komodakis*
Introduces a hierarchical continuous diffusion LM that jointly denoises token representations at multiple granularities, addressing the representation-design bottleneck that has limited diffusion LMs versus autoregressive ones.

**When Forgetting is not Catastrophic: On the Mechanics of Spurious Forgetting**
[arxiv.org/abs/2610.08718v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08718v1)
*Palit, Draye, Zucchet et al.*
Provides a mechanistic account of why finetuning-induced forgetting is often reversible — and sometimes reverses itself — challenging the assumption that lost recall reflects lost knowledge.

**Principled Under Pressure: Post-Training Decides Whether LLMs Act on Their Own Moral Judgment**
[arxiv.org/abs/2610.08670v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08670v1)
*Reblitz-Richardson*
A pre-registered 248-scenario panel across five pressure types separates stated values from enacted behavior, showing post-training — not base knowledge — determines whether agents act on their moral judgment.

**Towards In-Parameter Memory Augmentation for Large Language Models**
[arxiv.org/abs/2610.08630v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08630v1)
*Huang, Xie, Bai et al.*
Argues for encoding post-pretraining knowledge (domain facts, preferences, experience) directly into model parameters as a cheaper alternative to context-heavy ICL and external retrieval.

### 🤖 Agents & Reasoning

[arxiv.org/abs/2610.08775v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08775v1)

Defines "bottling" — the ability of an agent to autonomously produce cheap, reusable artifacts (e.g., distilled programs) that replace millions of repeated LLM calls — and evaluates whether current agents can do it.
