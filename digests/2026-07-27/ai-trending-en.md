**Enterprise Tooling Goes Open Source**: Alibaba's `open-code-review` (+832 today) mirrors a trend of major tech companies releasing internal AI tooling under open-source licenses. The hybrid deterministic + LLM architecture is particularly noteworthy—it acknowledges that pure LLM approaches aren't reliable enough for production code review without guardrails.
