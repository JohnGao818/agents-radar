## 2. Key Papers

### 🧠 Large Language Models (architecture, training, alignment, evaluation)

- **LoopAhead: Speculating Ahead for Faster Decoding in Looped Language Models** — [arXiv:2610.21594v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21594v1) — Zixuan Liu, Nan Xue, Rui Bao et al.  
  Extends pipelined self-speculative decoding to looped Transformers, targeting the repeated weight access that dominates latency in parameter-efficient looped architectures.

- **Waypoint-OPD: Transferring RL-Induced Policy Shifts via Gated On-Policy Distillation** — [arXiv:2610.21601v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21601v1) — Bingxi Hou, Guochao Jiang, Guofeng Quan et al.  
  Argues endpoint policy-shift distillation is insufficient and introduces intermediate "waypoint" supervision plus gating, making RL gains transferable to student models more faithfully.

- **When Does Muon Actually Win? A Paired Data-, Time-, and Tuning-Budget Benchmark** — [arXiv:2610.21555v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21555v1) — Mushir Akhtar, Hazem N. Nounou, Majdi Mansouri et al.  
  A single-A100, paired-comparison study across LMs and ViTs that asks the practically important question: under equal data, wall-clock, and tuning budgets, where does Muon's data efficiency survive?

- **When Is Muon Optimal? Radial Fragility and Minimax Geometry** — [arXiv:2610.21549v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21549v1) — Ao Xu  
  Provides a theoretical account (via the Isotropic Curvature Model) of when gradient orthogonalization minimizes one-step local loss, and where radial fragility breaks it.

### 🤖 Agents & Reasoning (planning, tool use, multi-agent, chain-of-thought)

  Moves evaluation beyond isolated inductive-reasoning or hypothesis-generation scores toward the full iterative inquiry cycle over complex systems.

- **Rethinking the Role of Tool Graph Memory in Multi-Turn Tool Use** — [arXiv:2610.21571v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21571v1) — Uiji Hwang, Richeng Xuan, Taeuk Kim  
  A welcome negative-result-style investigation into whether tool-transition memory consistently helps agents, rather than assuming it does.

- **QREF: Improving DeepSearch Agents by Aligning Search with Reasoning via Query Refinement and Evidence Filtering** — [arXiv:2610.21512v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21512v1) — Yujia Chen, Yuhui Wang, Junzhe Wang et al.  
  Directly attacks intent-misaligned and redundant queries plus accumulating retrieval noise, the two dominant failure sources in iterative search-and-reason agents.

- **OPRD: On-Policy Reminder Distillation for Long-Horizon Coding Agents** — [arXiv:2610.21496v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21496v1) — Yuechen Luo, Zhiyi Lai, Miao Ziqi et al.  
  Diagnostic analysis shows repository-level agents fail to act on information already in context, motivating on-policy reminders rather than more context or bigger models.
