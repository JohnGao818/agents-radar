**2. A Later Test Set Is Not a New Domain: Pretraining Familiarity Survives a Contamination-Free Hold-Out** — [arXiv:2609.10357v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10357v1)
*Moghadasi & Ghaderi*
Builds a post-training-cutoff benchmark for time-series foundation models and shows that temporal hold-outs alone do not eliminate pretraining familiarity — an important caution for the entire foundation-model evaluation ecosystem.

**3. A Dominant Diffuse Phase in the Sparse Autoencoder Phase Diagram** — [arXiv:2609.10299v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10299v1)
*Alexis D. Plascencia*
Systematically characterizes when SAEs absorb or merge co-occurring features, identifying a dominant diffuse phase and giving the interpretability community a controlled map of when feature recovery fails.

**4. CompassOPD: Cross-Family On-Policy Distillation via Within-Family Likelihood Shifts** — [arXiv:2609.10154v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10154v1)
*Gu et al.*
Diagnoses why on-policy distillation degrades across model families (even after tokenizer alignment) and proposes a within-family likelihood-shift signal that restores dense token-level supervision cross-family.

**5. Active Adaptation, Not Static Defense: Temporal Dynamics of Preventative Steering in Adversarial Fine-Tuning** — [arXiv:2609.10142v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10142v1)
*Guan et al.*
Shows that Preventative Steering's protection against harmful persona drift is not a fixed property but evolves over fine-tuning, reframing safety defenses as temporally dynamic processes rather than one-shot interventions.
